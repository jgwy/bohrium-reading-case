# Source and rights

Christoph Hafemeister and Rahul Satija. “Normalization and variance stabilization of single-cell RNA-seq data using regularized negative binomial regression.” Genome Biology 20, 296 (2019). Published version of record: 23 December 2019.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-019-1874-1
PMID: 31870423. PMCID: PMC6927181.

The only full original in this directory is materials/Hafemeister_Satija_2019_sctransform.pdf, the genuine 15-page publisher PDF used in the App. It is unchanged. A failed HTML download carrying a PDF extension is not included.

The paper's first page states © The Author(s) 2019 and Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 permission: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Retain author/source attribution and identify adaptations.

The App quotes and discusses this paper. Independent checks extracted text from the PDF, joined line-end hyphens and normalized whitespace/Unicode for quote matching. The App's answers are shipped as unedited accessibility exports (README sections 4 and 6 give the node ranges).
